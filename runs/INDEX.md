## Reading these numbers

* **PILOT** rows were produced on a 16 GB RTX 4080 SUPER under Option A of `docs/New_Submission/experiment_protocol.md` §9.5. They establish feasibility and catch defects. They are **not** confirmatory results and must not appear in a main or ablation table.
* `Invalid` is the share of predictions that are not a legal answer for their row. A high value means the run is measuring output formatting rather than perception — read it before reading the accuracy.
* All accuracies come from the repaired `evaluate.py`, which counts unanswered items as wrong and rejects duplicate question ids.
* `val` is the development split. `test` is evaluated once, after settings are locked (protocol §5).
